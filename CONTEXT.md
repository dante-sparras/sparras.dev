# sparras.dev

Dante Sparrås's personal site and portfolio: a single homepage presenting his profile, work and writing.

## Language

### Content

**Profile**:
The facts about Dante that the site presents: name, role, summary, Availability, bio, avatar, studies, location, time zone and Social links. The single source for anything that says who he is.
_Avoid_: bio data, about, identity

**Availability**:
What Dante is looking for and when: the LIA internship, its dates and where he can work.
_Avoid_: call to action, status, open to work

**Social link**:
A link to one of Dante's accounts elsewhere (GitHub, X, LinkedIn, Discord), shown with that platform's icon.
_Avoid_: socials, contact link

### Homepage structure

**Profile area**:
The fixed top of the homepage that introduces Dante: the banner, the Availability banner, the Profile overview and the About paragraph. It is not a Section and is not in the nav.
_Avoid_: hero, header section

**Section**:
A titled, navigable part of the homepage below the Profile area (Projects, References, Experience, Skills, Blog). A Section with nothing to show is left out of the page and the nav.
_Avoid_: block, panel, area

**Experience**:
A position Dante has held, shown under the organization with when he held it and what he did. Open positions are marked on the organization.
_Avoid_: resume, jobs, work history, career

**Homepage outline**:
The ordered list of Sections. The nav and the order of the page both come from it.
_Avoid_: sitemap, nav data, table of contents

**Project illustration**:
The 2:1 live scene on a Project's card, which plays while the card is hovered or has keyboard focus. In Winland, windows open, trade places and the workspace changes. In Winshell, three desktops that look nothing alike wipe into each other: a widget dashboard, a soft desktop with a floating dock and island, and a circular HUD. In sparras.dev, a capture of the homepage scrolls down to Projects and dives into its own card, landing on the homepage again.
_Avoid_: screenshot, thumbnail

### Pointer effects

**Pointer effect**:
A visual response on an element that tracks the visitor's pointer while it is over the element and settles back to rest once it leaves.
_Avoid_: hover effect, animation

**Hover lens**:
The soft circular window on the banner that reveals the alternate black-hole image under the pointer, slightly magnified.
_Avoid_: reveal, magnifier, spotlight

**RGB split**:
A chromatic offset in which an element's red and blue channels pull apart along the line from its center toward the pointer; used on the profile avatar and skill tiles.
_Avoid_: chromatic aberration, glitch, RGB shift
