# sparras.dev

Dante Sparrås's personal site and portfolio: a single homepage presenting his profile, work and writing.

## Language

### Content

**Profile**:
The facts about Dante that the site presents: name, role, summary, bio, avatar, company, location, time zone and Social links. The single source for anything that says who he is.
_Avoid_: bio data, about, identity

**Social link**:
A link to one of Dante's accounts elsewhere (GitHub, X, LinkedIn), shown with that platform's icon.
_Avoid_: socials, contact link

### Homepage structure

**Profile area**:
The fixed top of the homepage that introduces Dante: the banner, the Profile overview and the About paragraph. It is not a Section and is not in the nav.
_Avoid_: hero, header section

**Section**:
A titled, navigable part of the homepage below the Profile area (References, Skills, Projects, Blog). A Section with nothing to show is left out of the page and the nav.
_Avoid_: block, panel, area

**Homepage outline**:
The ordered list of Sections. The nav and the order of the page both come from it.
_Avoid_: sitemap, nav data, table of contents

### Pointer effects

**Pointer effect**:
A visual response on an element that tracks the visitor's pointer while it is over the element and settles back to rest once it leaves.
_Avoid_: hover effect, animation

**Hover lens**:
The soft circular window on the banner that reveals the alternate black-hole image under the pointer, slightly magnified.
_Avoid_: reveal, magnifier, spotlight

**RGB split**:
A chromatic offset in which an element's red and blue channels pull apart along the line from its center toward the pointer; used on the profile avatar and skill tiles.
_Avoid_: chromatic aberration, glitch, RGB shift
